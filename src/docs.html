1. Executive Overview & Core Problem Statement

Traditional workforce management systems suffer from disconnected silos: employee background verification data lives in static reports, while daily attendance is locked inside standalone biometric hardware. When an employee transitions between companies, their verified identity is lost, and their historical attendance reliability cannot be verified.

The Universal Employee Verification & Attendance System solves this by establishing a Universal Barcode Identity:

  • Permanent Cryptographic Barcode Pass: Generated once per employee, encoding their unique system identifier.
  • Company-Level Permission Management: A company grants active access when onboarding and revokes access upon entering a permanent, unmodifiable Last Date.
  • Multi-Tenure Re-Activation: When an employee moves to their next company, the new company activates the exact same universal barcode pass, creating a unified cross-company career record.
  • Lifetime Month-Wise Attendance Audits: Companies can scan the barcode pass to audit verified monthly counts for Total Present, Total Absent, and Permissions & Leaves across the entire tenure.

2. High-Level System Architecture & Flow

End-to-End Information & Event Flow
1
Identity Generation & Verification Store: Admin registers employee demographics, salary history, and salary slip PDF. System creates records in PostgreSQL (Person & PersonDetails) and renders a Code 128 / QR digital pass.
2
Company Attendance Kiosk (Daily Clock-In/Out): Employee presents their universal barcode to the camera kiosk. The optical scanner decodes the barcode, validates company permission, logs Check-In / Check-Out, triggers a 3-second visual cooldown, and broadcasts live scans via WebSocket to the live activity stream.
3
Lifecycle Offboarding & Permanent Lock: When an employee departs, the company enters their Last Date. Once submitted, the field is permanently locked, and the barcode is switched to inactive (403 Forbidden on attendance punch).
4
Next Company Onboarding: The subsequent company looks up the existing barcode pass, inputs their new joining record (start date, role, department), and reactivates the same universal barcode for their company facility.
5
Lifetime Attendance Analytics Audit: Any authorized company scans or searches the barcode to query PostgreSQL, grouping attendance punch history by month and displaying verified monthly Present, Absent, and Permission rates.

3. Module 01: Universal Barcode Identity & Generation

The generation engine converts verified demographics into a scannable Code 128 standard 1D barcode and 2D QR badge.

Attribute Specification Purpose
Symbology Code 128 (High-Density Alphanumeric) & QR Code Ensures instant readability on all 1D laser scanners and webcams.
Universal Payload EMP-XXX or Cryptographic Hash Resolves to the employee's immutable primary key in the database.
Badge Components Photo, Universal Code, Active Company, Role, Salary Slip URL Printable digital credential for on-site visual and hardware verification.

4. Module 02: Real-Time Optical Attendance Kiosk

The kiosk module turns any laptop, tablet, or USB camera into an enterprise-grade biometric barcode terminal.

Key Kiosk Capabilities:
  • Smart Toggle Check-In / Check-Out: First scan of the day logs Check-In; subsequent scans log Check-Out and compute exact shift duration.
  • Foreign Barcode Rejection: Only barcodes generated by this software are validated; random retail barcodes are rejected immediately.
  • 3-Second Visual Cooldown Overlay: Prevents duplicate scanning loops and displays employee name, photo, and time badge.
  • Real-Time WebSocket Stream: Live scans appear instantly in the manager activity console without page reloads.

5. Module 03: Barcode Permission Lifecycle (Locking & Re-Activation)

Addresses strict enterprise governance for employee joining, offboarding, and career transitions:

Lifecycle Stage System Action Database & Permission State
1. New Onboarding Admin assigns barcode + Start Date is_active = true, start_date = DATE, end_date = NULL. Active for attendance.
2. Offboarding Admin clicks "Enter Last Date" end_date = DATE, is_active = false. Button permanently replaced with locked badge ( Last: YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Access Revoked Offboarded employee scans barcode Terminal returns 403 Access Denied ("Barcode permission revoked or expired").
4. Next Company New company activates barcode Creates new PersonDetails entry with new joining date and company facility, reactivating pass.

6. Module 04: Lifetime Company Attendance (Month-Wise Breakdown)

Allows companies to audit an employee's lifetime performance at their facility with month-by-month breakdowns:

Month-Wise Audit Parameters:
Total Present Verified physical kiosk check-ins and remote punch records for the month.
Total Absent Unexcused absences and recorded leave days during the working period.
Permissions & Leaves Late arrivals, approved permission slips, and half-day authorizations.
Reliability Rate Calculated attendance percentage: (Present / Total Working Days) * 100.

8. REST API Specification & Endpoint Directory

Method Endpoint Functionality
POST /api/v1/persons Registers new employee, creates demographics & employment history.
GET /api/v1/persons Retrieves all registered employees with relational employment histories.
POST /api/v1/persons/:id/deactivate Offboards employee by recording unmodifiable end_date and setting is_active=false.
POST /api/v1/attendance/barcode-punch Validates barcode pass, checks active company permissions, records check-in/out.
POST /api/v1/attendance/employee-lifetime-analytics Aggregates lifetime punch logs into month-wise Present, Absent, and Permission metrics.
GET /api/v1/attendance/records Fetches workforce attendance punch history with durations.
DELETE /api/v1/attendance/records/:id Removes specific attendance log entry.
